# Zenda Health Disclaimer

**Version 1.0 — Effective 2 July 2026**

Please read this carefully. It is short on purpose.

## Zenda is not medical advice

Zenda is a fitness and nutrition coaching app. Its plans, reports, coach notes, and
estimates — including everything generated by AI — are for general fitness purposes
only. They are **not** medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and Zenda is not a
medical device.

## Talk to a doctor first

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting this or any exercise or
nutrition programme, especially if any of these apply:

- you have, or suspect, any medical condition (heart, metabolic, joint, or other);
- you are pregnant, postpartum, or breastfeeding;
- you are recovering from an injury or surgery;
- you take medication that affects exercise, appetite, or hydration;
- you are over 60 or have been inactive for a long time.

Zenda asks about conditions and injuries to make plans more sensible — but it
cannot examine you, and it cannot clear you for exercise. Only a professional can.

## Listen to your body

Stop exercising immediately and seek medical attention if you experience chest
pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, fainting, or sharp pain. Soreness is
normal; pain is a signal.

## AI content can be wrong

Zenda's coaching is AI-generated. It can be inaccurate or unsuitable for your
specific situation, even when it sounds confident. Calorie and macro figures are
estimates, not measurements. **Never rely on an AI estimate for allergen safety** —
always check labels and your own tolerances.

## Not for eating-disorder treatment

Zenda involves tracking food, weight, and body photos. It is not designed for, and
must not be used as, support or treatment for eating disorders or disordered
eating. If tracking harms your relationship with food or your body, please stop
using the app and reach out to a professional or a local support line.

## Your responsibility

You use Zenda's plans and suggestions voluntarily, at your own risk, and within
your own limits. When in doubt — about a movement, a target, or how you feel —
choose caution and ask a professional.
